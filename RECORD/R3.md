# R3 — the conformance theorem

> Moved verbatim from `PLAN.md` §23.1 (lines 2094–2099) on 2026-09-25.

- **R3 — the conformance theorem**: the core is a `Conforms` instance, so its proofs are the layer proofs the frame and connection instances already established rather than a new development. **What that proves is narrower than "the endpoint is correct", and the difference is worth stating because it is easy to overread**: the core is a *driver* over `Spec.Connection.step`, so it shares the specification's decision function by construction and R3's content is the **plumbing** — unit extraction, buffering, direction, output order, rendering, and the api reading — not the protocol decisions. A defect inside `Spec.Connection.step` is inherited rather than caught, and finding it is the corpus's and the differential's work. **And R3 is discharged**: `Proofs/EndpointConformance.lean` proves `Contracts/EndpointConformance.lean`'s statement at exactly the type it froze, and `Contracts/EndpointAcceptance.lean` binds it
(`endpoint_conformance_public`). The proof was the plumbing as predicted — three of the four equalities relating the two independently written constructions closed by `rfl`, the fourth needing a case split only because
`Except.toOption` is opaque to the default transparency — and **its value is that the two plumbings were written separately and this is what says they agree**: the implementation's `arriving`/`sending`/`answerOf`/`submissionOf`
against the specification's `specArriving`/`specSending`/`specAnswerOf`/`specSubmissionOf`. **Doing it exposed two holes in the proof-integrity gate, both now fixed**: its presence check grepped only for `' depends on axioms`, so a
theorem resting on *no* axioms — the strongest case, and two of the four equalities are exactly that — could never be inventoried; and its printed inventory filtered on the same single form, so those entries were checked and then
hidden from the reader the inventory exists for. Independence between two *readings* of the standard already exists and is bought, in `lean/Ref/` and the differential and mutation tiers over it; what the endpoint uniquely adds is a process that speaks the protocol over a socket, not a third reading.
